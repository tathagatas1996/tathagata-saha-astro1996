About

I am an observational astronomer primarily studying systems powered by accretion onto compact objects - how these systems affect their immediate environment and host galaxy, and how they are shaped by astrophysical processes such as mergers, tidal interactions, and star formation. My research spans a broad range of accretion-powered systems, from stellar-mass X-ray binaries and their evolution in the context of the host stellar population, to transient accretion onto supermassive black holes in the form of tidal disruption events and changing-look AGNs.
